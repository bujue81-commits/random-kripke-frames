## Incremental checkpoint: matching existence-probability exponent

- 94259a75450aa91e7ef335260a58a84e7db393ca: proofs/CHAIN_SHARP_EXISTENCE_RATE.md closes the previous gap by a balanced-map second-moment argument. For fixed r>=2 and L=log_2 m, the internal hand theorem is log_2 p_m=-(r-1)^2 L^2+O_r(L log L). Here p_m is the probability of existence of a surjective bounded morphism onto the explicitly defined chain target. The previous first-moment proof gives the upper bound; the new argument gives the matching lower bound. This supersedes the interval for the limiting exponent in the preceding checkpoint.
- Key proof: restrict to maps with each noncentral fibre of size a=ceil L, total s=ra. If f and g differ by k vertices of their noncentral supports, their forbidden ordered-coordinate sets H_f,H_g, each of cardinal Q=(r^2-r+1)a^2, satisfy |H_f intersect H_g|<=Q-ak. Indeed all within-fibre ordered pairs are forbidden, and deleting k vertices from the support removes at least ak of these coordinates. Thus joint success probability is <=2^(-Q-ak). For each f there are at most binom(s,k) binom(m-s,k) r^s such g; summing with m*2^(-a)<=1 gives E[Y^2]<=N 2^(-Q)(2r)^s, where Y counts successful balanced maps and N counts the candidate maps. Since each full success probability is >=c_r 2^(-Q), Cauchy-Schwarz gives p_m>=c_r^2 N 2^(-Q)(2r)^(-s), with c_r=exp(-2(r+1)). This matches the logarithmic first-moment exponent. No independence between different maps is assumed.
